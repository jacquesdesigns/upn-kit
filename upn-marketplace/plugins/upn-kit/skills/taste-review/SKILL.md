# Taste review

Read `<home>/taste/REVERSAL-LOG.md`, `<home>/taste/LESSONS.md` and `<home>/taste/STANCE.md` (`home` is in
`~/.claude/upn-kit/config.json`).

1. **Group the reversals** by what they were about (spacing, type, colour, imagery, motion, structure,
   copy, "generic"). Count each group and quote two or three rows in the person's words.
2. **Name the patterns plainly:** "You have overturned the judge on spacing 6 times, always asking for
   more air", "Every hero the judge passed as bold, you called loud".
3. **Offer, never apply.** For each pattern, offer one option: add a line to a specific project's
   rubric, add a sentence to STANCE.md in their words, or leave it as it is. Taste stays with the person;
   nothing becomes a rule unless they say so, in their words.
4. **Lessons:** list any lesson that has happened twice, and ask if it should change how they work.

Keep it short: patterns, quotes, the choices. British or American spelling to match how the person writes.
